Wedding planning timeline calculator

Most wedding checklists assume a 12-month engagement. Enter your own wedding date and the day you start: every task gets a real date. If you have less than a year, the timeline is squeezed in the same order instead of leaving the first months of the list overdue, and tasks that are already tight are flagged so you book them first.

Calendar file: one all-day event per task on its date, works with Google Calendar, Apple Calendar and Outlook (import). Nothing is sent anywhere, the page runs in your browser.

How the dates are set

Each task has two lead times: the usual one (how long before the wedding couples typically do it, for example 12 months for the venue) and the latest sensible one (for example 6 weeks for mailing invitations). These are common planning conventions, not data from a survey: popular venues, photographers and caterers often book a year ahead in peak season, much less off-season.

Squeeze (default): when you have less time than the longest lead time, every usual lead time is scaled down by the same ratio, so the order stays the same, but no task is moved later than its latest sensible date. Keep ideal dates: tasks whose usual date has passed are all put on your start date and marked late, the rest keep the classic dates.

A task is flagged tight when your start date is already past its latest sensible date: do it in the first days, and expect fewer choices.
